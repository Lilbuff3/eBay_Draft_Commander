## 2024-05-14 - Accessible Drag-and-Drop Thumbnails and Hover States
**Learning:** Sortable thumbnail elements (like those powered by `@dnd-kit`) are inherently interactive and require full accessibility compliance just like standard buttons. Furthermore, navigation elements that use hover-only visibility (e.g. `sm:group-hover:opacity-100`) become invisible to keyboard users who navigate via `Tab`.
**Action:** When implementing sortable thumbnail items, ensure the root `<button>` includes an explicit `aria-label` identifying the image, an `aria-pressed` state to indicate selection, and `focus-visible` styling. For interactive elements hidden on mobile or untargeted by a mouse, combine the hover reveal pattern (`sm:group-hover:opacity-100`) with keyboard focus visibility (`sm:focus-visible:opacity-100`).
