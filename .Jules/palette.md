## 2024-05-15 - Missing ARIA labels in Data Grid Actions
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons in trailing 'Actions' columns of table or data grid components are frequently missing descriptive `aria-label` attributes, leading to a confusing experience for screen reader users who only hear 'button' without context.
**Action:** When reviewing table or data grid components, specifically check the trailing 'Actions' column for icon-only buttons to ensure they have descriptive `aria-label` attributes to maintain screen reader accessibility.
