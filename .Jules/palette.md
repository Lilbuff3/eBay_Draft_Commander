## 2024-05-18 - Missing ARIA Labels on Icon-Only Modal Buttons
**Learning:** Across Draft Commander's modal and panel components (e.g., `PreviewPanel.tsx`, `MigrationModal.tsx`, `TemplateManager.tsx`, `PriceResearch.tsx`), icon-only buttons (like those with `size="icon"` for close or zoom actions) frequently lack `aria-label` or `title` attributes, making them inaccessible to screen readers and lacking tooltips for mouse users.
**Action:** When creating or reviewing new modal or utility panel components, specifically check all buttons using `<Button size="icon">` to ensure they have an explicit `aria-label` describing their action and a `title` for tooltip support. Also ensure that inline input fields and list row checkboxes include appropriate `aria-label` attributes tying them to their specific item or purpose.
