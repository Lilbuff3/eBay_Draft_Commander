# Domain Context: eBay Draft Commander

## Glossary

### Clean Listing
An eBay listing created with structured Item Specifics and minimalist, mobile-first scannable bullet points (Item Overview, Condition & Testing, Included, Key Specs), containing zero HTML tables, inline styles, or embedded photo duplicates. Guaranteed to be 100% editable inside the native eBay mobile app.

### Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
The acquisition cost paid by the seller for an item. Used to calculate true net profit and establish minimum pricing/offer floors.

### Everything Seller
A reseller who sources and lists diverse inventory across arbitrary categories (electronics, clothing, vintage, media, tools, collectibles) where item condition, completeness, and attributes vary per item.

### Markdown Ladder
An automated, compounding price reduction schedule triggered when an active listing reaches a configured aging threshold without selling, designed to liquidate stale capital.

### Mobile Review Card
A streamlined, mobile-first preview screen displaying an item's thumbnail, AI-generated title, comp price, calculated net profit margin, and shipping tier, enabling a 5-second human sanity check and 1-tap publishing.

### Net Margin
The projected or realized profit from a sale, calculated as: `Gross Sale Price - COGS - eBay Final Value Fees - Estimated Shipping Cost`.

### Outlier Floor
A protective price threshold calculated during AI comp research to prevent high-value, rare, or vintage items from being underpriced.

### Seller Note
Optional free-form text or voice input provided by the seller at capture time (e.g. "Tested working, tape deck needs belt, paid $5") that is ingested alongside image photos during AI vision analysis.

### Stale Inventory
Active listings that remain unsold past an aging threshold (e.g., 30, 60, or 90 days).
